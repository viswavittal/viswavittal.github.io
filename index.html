Overview

About

Computational chemist specialising in molecular dynamics simulations, multiscale modelling, and protein-surface interactions. Doctoral research focuses on synthetic blood-brain barrier models — combining fully atomistic and MARTINI coarse-grained MD to understand how fibronectin domains adsorb onto SAM surfaces, informing next-generation neurological implant design.

Research Focus

  • MD simulations of protein-surface interactions
  • Coarse-grained modelling (MARTINI / GROMACS)
  • Structure-based drug design & docking
  • Blood-brain barrier biomaterial modelling
  • ADMET profiling & drug-likeness analysis
